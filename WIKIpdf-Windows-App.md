## Built for technical documents

Finding a component or specification across many PDF drawings and equipment manuals can be slow. WIKIpdf is a Windows desktop app for viewing and working with PDFs, with optional AI-assisted document chat and Wiki generation to organize reusable knowledge.

### Confirmed features from the public Store listing

- View PDF documents, annotate and print.
- Optional Codex-powered chat and document analysis through your own connected AI account.
- Optional Wiki generation to help organize document knowledge.

**Important:** AI functionality depends on separate provider account access; some functionality may involve in-app purchases. Free download does not mean every feature is free. Check availability and requirements in the Microsoft Store listing before installing.

## Who might find it useful?

- Marine engineers searching equipment and vessel manuals.
- Maintenance technicians referencing PDF documentation.
- Mechanical, plant and manufacturing engineers working with technical drawings.
- Anyone managing many PDFs who wants to explore a personal document Wiki.
